    How this was worked out, and what it leaves out
    • Prices are Yahoo Finance daily closes, adjusted for splits but not for dividends or rights issues.
    • US index: the Nasdaq-100 (NDX). Thai index: the TDEX fund, which tracks SET50, because Yahoo returns no history for the SET index itself.
    • A pullback runs from an index closing high to its lowest close before the next new high, and counts if that fall is 5% or more. The Thai series starts on 20 Nov 2024, so its first pullback began earlier than shown.
    • Downside beta is the slope of a stock's daily return against the index's on days the index fell. "Beat the index" means the stock's return from the index peak to its low was higher than the index's.
    • Stocks with under 60 days of history are left out (—). Newer listings such as GULF (merged April 2025), TIDLOR and THAI (relisted August 2025) have shorter histories, so fewer pullbacks count for them.
    • The data updates automatically after each Thai and US close. The stress test applies past betas to a fall you choose; it is not a prediction.
    • Sectors are broad groups chosen for this study, not official GICS or SET classifications.

    Data: Yahoo Finance daily closes, refreshed automatically after the Thai and US market closes. Not financial advice. Source code: kaorii-ako/the-lookout.
